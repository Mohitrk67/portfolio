About Me

Unlocking insights from structured datasets.

I am a Data Analyst with a background in Engineering Management and Civil Engineering. My expertise lies in bridging physical operations with analytical data pipelines. I design interactive dashboards, write optimized SQL queries, and construct predictive scripts to identify trends, mitigate stockout risks, and optimize processes.

My work combines technical data analysis with operational excellence principles:

  • Quantitative BI: Constructing clean data models and dynamic DAX metrics to track student participation, construction delays, and return logistics.
  • Process Optimization: Applying project management methodologies to eliminate tracking errors, speed up inspection workflows, and reduce project rework.
  • AI and Automation: Implementing AI-powered retrievals and language processing to make business intelligence queries natural and accessible.
